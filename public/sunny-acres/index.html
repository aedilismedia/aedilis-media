Is Sunny Acres family friendly?

Yes! Sunny Acres is rated for everyone. Everyone is welcome. Everyone stays.

Full controller support on every platform, plus mouse and keyboard. You can also just watch.

About 60 hours for the main story. Most players never finish. Most players never stop.

Yes. He can still see you when you're offline.
